EUNIS habitat classification (2012) · EUNIS-Habitatklassifikation (2012) · lkg:authority_eunis

Habitat concepts (lkg:habitatScheme). Grade from the model/reviewer judgement (exact, close, broad). EUNIS records also carry their skos:broader chain up to the level-1 group and rdfs:seeAlso to the Eionet Data Dictionary and BISE pages.

Open scheme — concepts are minted in the data (no fixed list).

GBIF Backbone Taxonomy · GBIF-Backbone-Taxonomie · lkg:authority_gbif

Taxa (lkg:Taxon). Grade: exactMatch = GBIF EXACT match at or above the configured confidence; closeMatch = FUZZY match or a name proposed by the LLM and verified in GBIF; broadMatch = HIGHERRANK (the name is anchored at genus level). Mirrored as dwc:taxonID (except broadMatch).

Open scheme — concepts are minted in the data (no fixed list).

GeoNames · GeoNames · lkg:authority_geonames

Places (lkg:Place). Grade: exactMatch = the OpenStreetMap hit and the GeoNames record agree, or a reviewer confirmed the link; closeMatch = single-source name match (GeoNames alone, unique in the home region).

Open scheme — concepts are minted in the data (no fixed list).

Habitats · Lebensräume · lkg:habitatScheme

Open concept scheme collecting the habitat concepts emitted as data (skos:Concept nodes such as Auwald, Schilf, Moor, linked from observations via dwciri:habitat); not enumerated here. Since ontology 0.4.3 each concept carries at most one skos:exactMatch / skos:closeMatch / skos:broadMatch to a class of the EUNIS habitat classification (2012; Eionet vocabulary http://eunis.eea.europa.eu/eunishabitats/<code>, e.g. Auwald → G1.2, Schilf → C3.21, Garten → I2.2); the EUNIS classes are in the data graph as authority records of lkg:authority_eunis (skos:notation, skos:prefLabel, their skos:broader chain and rdfs:seeAlso links to the maintained pages — Eionet Data Dictionary, BISE 2012 hierarchical view; the eunis.eea.europa.eu application itself is retired).

Open scheme — concepts are minted in the data (no fixed list).
